### The EFC Gatekeeper

EFC serves as the critical "gatekeeper" system:

1. **Performs CBRS spectrum scans** at 6 stadium locations
2. **Blocks wireless systems** (C2P, WiFi, O2O) until spectrum is clear
3. **Prevents competitive violations** from RF interference
4. **Documents evidence** through WhatsApp photos and timestamps

**If EFC fails**: $500K-$2M+ potential liability exposure
