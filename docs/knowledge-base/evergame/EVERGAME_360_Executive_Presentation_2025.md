### Dynamic Task Management with Automatic Impact Analysis

#### The Challenge

- ❌ Manual tracking of certification impacts
- ❌ Risk of deploying uncertified personnel
- ❌ Weeks to update training materials
- ❌ No visibility into who needs retraining

#### The EVERGAME 360 Solution

**AI-Powered Certification Impact Analysis**
When any task is added, modified, or deleted:

1. **Automatic Analysis** (powered by Claude AI)
   - Identifies affected certification modules
   - Assesses training gap for existing GDAs
   - Calculates re-certification scope (delta vs full)
   - Generates risk assessment report
   - **Duration: <60 seconds**

2. **Intelligent Training Determination**
   ```
   Scenario 1: New procedural task added
   → Impact: DELTA TRAINING (15-30 min micro-module)

   → Timeline: Complete before next assignment
   
   Scenario 2: Critical system operation modified
   → Impact: FULL RECERTIFICATION (4-8 hours)
   → Action: Schedule mandatory retraining
   → Timeline: Complete before any game assignment
   
   Scenario 3: Documentation update only
   → Impact: NO TRAINING REQUIRED
   → Action: Email notification with updated playbook
   → Timeline: Immediate deployment
   ```

3. **Automated Compliance Enforcement**
   - ✅ GDA cannot be assigned unless ACTIVE certification
   - ✅ System blocks scheduling of uncertified personnel

   - ✅ Supervisor alerts for upcoming games with cert gaps
