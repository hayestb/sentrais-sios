## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Is this replacing our existing Agile PMO framework?**  
A: SIPE *enhances* Agile PMO by adding intelligence, prediction, and automation. It operationalizes the NIN methodology within an Agile delivery model. Your Jira workflows, sprint ceremonies, and governance structures remain—SIPE just makes them smarter.

**Q: How much of my time is required?**  
A: As CEO, approximately **5 hours per week** for the first 90 days (strategic guidance, stakeholder management, monthly reviews). After launch, **2-3 hours per month** for quarterly reviews and governance.

**Q: What if we don't have all the technical resources (Data Engineer, ML expertise)?**  
A: Phase 1 can start with simpler rule-based systems (no ML required initially). You can hire/contract ML expertise for Phase 2, or partner with a data science consultancy for the first 6 months until you build internal capability.

**Q: How do we prevent SIPE from becoming "shelfware" like other systems?**  
A: Three mechanisms: (1) Tie performance incentives to SIPE compliance, (2) Make it so useful that teams *want* to use it (not forced), (3) Self-learning engine continuously improves based on feedback, so it gets better over time, not worse.

**Q: What's the single most important thing to get right in first 90 days?**  
A: **Churn prevention in Diagnose/Design phases**. If you solve this (30% reduction target), everything else follows—higher client satisfaction, better margins, faster scaling, easier recruiting (teams want to work on successful projects).
